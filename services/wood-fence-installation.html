Why Choose a Wood Fence for Your Austin or Buda Home?

Wood fencing gives you something metal and vinyl can't — a natural, warm look that fits every yard style from traditional to modern. But looks alone don't justify the investment. Here's why wood makes sense for Central Texas homeowners:

Full privacy for your family

A solid wood privacy fence creates a complete visual barrier — no gaps, no sightlines into your yard. Your kids and pets play safely. You relax without an audience.

Built for Texas weather

Western Red Cedar's natural oils resist rot, insects, and moisture — the exact conditions that destroy inferior fence materials in our climate. The right build outlasts what most people expect.

Fully customizable

Height, style, stain color, cap trim, gate placement — every detail is built to your property. No stock panels, no compromises on layout.

Adds real property value

A quality cedar fence with a clean stain and proper gate installation adds curb appeal and increases property value — especially in Central Texas neighborhoods where outdoor living matters.

Why We Build with Western Red Cedar — Not Pressure-Treated Pine

We get this question all the time. Pressure-treated pine is cheaper upfront — so why don't we use it? Because we've installed thousands of fences in Central Texas, and we know what holds up and what doesn't.

Our Choice
Standard Material

🌲 Western Red Cedar

Naturally rot-resistant without chemicals. Cedar contains aromatic oils that repel insects and resist moisture — no treatment required. It stays straighter, holds stain better, and ages to a beautiful silver-gray if left natural.

  • Natural rot & insect resistance
  • Dimensional stability — stays straight
  • Holds stain significantly better
  • No chemical treatment needed
  • 20–25 year lifespan when properly maintained
  • Higher upfront material cost than pine

Pressure-Treated Pine

Pine requires chemical treatment to resist rot. The treatment works initially, but in Central Texas's intense UV exposure and clay soil moisture, treated pine tends to warp, crack, and twist within a few years — especially as it dries out after installation.

  • Lower upfront material cost
  • Prone to warping and cracking as it dries
  • Holds stain poorly — looks rough quickly
  • Chemical treatment can bleed green tint
  • Shorter effective lifespan in Texas climate

We use Western Red Cedar on every fence we build. It's the material we'd choose for our own homes.

Popular Cedar Fence Styles in Austin & Buda

We build any style of wood fence you can envision — custom cuts, heights, cap trim, and gate placements all handled in-house. Here are the most popular styles our Austin-area customers choose:

Privacy Fence (Vertical Board)

Pickets placed side-by-side create a solid, gap-free barrier. The most popular choice for backyard privacy and security. Clean, timeless, works with every yard style.

Board-on-Board Fence

Overlapping pickets on alternating sides create a completely solid screen that looks finished from both sides — ideal for corner lots or anywhere the fence is visible from the street.

Horizontal Cedar Fence

Boards run horizontally instead of vertically for a clean, modern look. Popular with contemporary and transitional home styles across Austin. Requires proper steel post installation to prevent sagging over time.

Shadow Box Fence

Alternating pickets on both sides of the rails create a semi-private fence with airflow and visual interest from both sides. A great middle ground between full privacy and an open look.

Picture Frame / Cap & Trim

Decorative trim added around each fence section or along the top creates a polished, finished look. Adds curb appeal and a premium feel to any style.

Frequently Asked Questions — Wood Fence Installation

How long does a cedar fence last in Texas?

A well-built Western Red Cedar fence lasts 20–25 years in the Central Texas climate. Cedar's natural oils resist rot and insects without any chemical treatment. Pairing cedar pickets with PostMaster steel posts (which we recommend) eliminates post rot at the soil line — the most common reason fences fail early in our clay-heavy soil.

Why Western Red Cedar and not pressure-treated pine?

Cedar is naturally rot-resistant, stays straighter as it ages, and holds stain dramatically better than pine. Treated pine can warp and crack as it dries out in Texas heat, and it absorbs stain unevenly. We've installed thousands of fences in Central Texas — cedar consistently outperforms pine over the long run. It's the material we'd choose for our own homes.

How long does wood fence installation take?

Most residential wood fence projects are completed in one day. We arrive, demolish and remove your old fence, install posts, build the fence, and do a final walkthrough — all before we leave. Large or complex projects may require two days. We'll give you a clear timeline when we provide the estimate.

Do I need to stain my wood fence?

Staining is optional but strongly recommended, especially in Central Texas. We use Ready Seal — an oil-based penetrating stain that protects cedar from UV damage and moisture without lap-marking in the heat. Apply it within the first year and reapply every 2–3 seasons. It meaningfully extends fence life and keeps the wood looking sharp. We offer Ready Seal staining as an add-on service to any installation.

What are PostMaster steel posts and why do you recommend them?

PostMaster is a galvanized steel sleeve post system set in concrete. In Buda's clay soil, moisture wicks into wood posts and causes rot at the base — often within 3–5 years. Steel posts eliminate this entirely and can last 30+ years. We recommend PostMaster on every wood fence installation. It's an upgrade over wood posts, not something included in the base price — but in our honest opinion, it's the single best investment you can make in fence longevity.

Do you remove and haul away old fences?

Yes — old fence demo and haul-away is included in all installation quotes at no extra charge. We remove the old fence, dispose of it properly, and leave your property clean.

Do you handle permits?

Yes. We handle all permits required by your municipality and contact 811 to have underground utility lines marked before any digging begins. You don't have to deal with the paperwork.

What is your 5-year workmanship warranty?

Every wood fence installation is backed by our 5-year workmanship warranty. If something isn't right due to our workmanship, we come back and fix it at no charge. This covers installation quality and structural integrity — not natural weathering of the wood, which is why we recommend staining.
