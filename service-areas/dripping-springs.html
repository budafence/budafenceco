Popular Fence Styles in Dripping Springs

Hill Country homeowners appreciate fences that blend with the natural landscape while providing privacy and security. Here are the most popular styles we install:

Cedar Privacy Fence

The natural choice for Hill Country homes. Western Red Cedar's warm tones complement the landscape. Starting at $30/LF with wood posts, $33/LF with steel posts.

Decorative Metal Fence

Perfect for front yards and properties where you want to preserve Hill Country views. Elegant flat-top or spear-top designs that don't obstruct the scenery.

Horizontal Cedar

Modern, contemporary look increasingly popular in Dripping Springs' newer communities. Clean lines that complement Hill Country architecture. Starting at $39/LF with steel posts.

Ranch & Agricultural Fencing

For working ranches and large acreage. Pipe fencing, game fencing for deer management, and traditional ranch-style wood fencing for any acreage size.
