Why Our Fences Outlast the Competition

Any contractor can nail boards to posts. What we use — and recommend — is what separates a 5-year fence from a 20-year fence.

Standard
🌲

Western Red Cedar Only

We never substitute pressure-treated pine. Western Red Cedar is naturally rot-resistant, insect-resistant, and holds stain dramatically better under Central Texas UV.

Recommended
🔩

PostMaster Steel Posts

Galvanized steel sleeve posts set in concrete. Buda's clay soil wicks moisture and destroys wood posts in 3–5 years. Steel posts last 30+ years and keep the fence plumb.

Recommended
🖌️

Ready Seal Staining

Oil-based, deep-penetrating stain that won't lap-mark in Texas heat. Applied every 2–3 seasons, it extends fence life by years and keeps cedar looking sharp.

Recommended
🚪

Welded Metal Gate Frames

Wood-framed gates sag and rack within a year. Welded metal frames hold alignment for the life of the fence. Available as an upgrade on any gate installation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers from your local Buda fence contractor — no runaround, no sales pitch.

How much does a cedar fence cost in Austin and Buda, TX?

Fence costs vary based on material, linear footage, terrain, number of gates, and site conditions. Every property is different, which is why we offer free estimates — call, text, or email today to schedule yours. We'll walk your property, measure the line, and hand you a written quote the same day as the estimate. Call or text (512) 710-7116 or request one online.

How long does fence installation take?

Most residential projects are completed in one day. We show up on time, install the fence, haul away your old fence, clean up the site, and do a final walkthrough with you before we leave. Large or complex projects may take two days — we'll tell you upfront so there are no surprises.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes — free, no-obligation estimates on all projects. We typically provide estimates the same day you call. Walk your property with us, get a written quote with exact materials and cost, and decide from there with zero pressure. Call (512) 710-7116 or fill out our contact form.

Are you licensed and insured?

Texas does not require a state license for fence contractors. We carry $1,000,000 in liability insurance and back every fence installation with a 5-year workmanship warranty. If something isn't right due to our workmanship, we come back and fix it at no charge.

Do you repair existing fences?

Yes. Fence repair is one of our core services — including post replacement with discounts for multiple posts. We repair storm damage, rotted posts, broken boards, sagging gates, and leaning fence lines across all of Central Texas. We'll always give you an honest assessment of whether repair or replacement makes more financial sense for your situation.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Buda, TX and serve all communities within approximately 25 miles — including Austin, Kyle, Dripping Springs, San Marcos, Wimberley, Driftwood, Westlake Hills, New Braunfels, and all surrounding Central Texas communities. Call to confirm your area — we're happy to help.
