Our Story

Buda Fence Company was founded in 2015 by William Espich, a Buda native who got tired of watching homeowners overpay for fences built with cheap materials and zero follow-through. What started as a one-man operation has grown into a trusted team serving all of Central Texas.

Over the years, we've completed more than 5,000 fence installations and built our reputation on one thing: doing it right the first time. We only use Western Red Cedar pickets — not pressure-treated pine. We set our posts in galvanized steel PostMaster sleeves, not bare wood that rots in Buda's clay soil. We finish with Ready Seal when staining. Every detail matters.

We're based in Buda, TX and proudly serve homeowners across Buda, Austin, Kyle, and Dripping Springs. Whether you need a new cedar privacy fence, a clean metal fence, or reliable fence repairs, you'll get straight answers and zero runaround.

Why Our Fences Outlast the Competition

Any contractor can nail boards to posts. Here's what separates a Buda Fence Company installation from everyone else.

Standard
🌲

Western Red Cedar Only

We never use pressure-treated pine. Western Red Cedar is naturally rot-resistant, insect-resistant, and holds stain far better in Central Texas heat.

Recommended
🔩

PostMaster Steel Posts

Galvanized steel sleeve posts set in concrete. Buda's clay soil wicks moisture and destroys wood posts in 3–5 years. Steel posts last decades.

Recommended
🖌️

Ready Seal Staining

Oil-based, deep-penetrating stain that protects against UV and won't lap-mark in Texas heat. Extends fence life by years when applied every 2–3 seasons.

Recommended
🚪

Welded Metal Gate Frames

We recommend welded metal frames for all gates — they hold alignment far better than wood-framed gates, which sag and rack within a year. Available as an upgrade on any project.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions from homeowners across Buda, Austin, Kyle, San Marcos, Dripping Springs, and surrounding Central Texas.

What types of fences does Buda Fence Company install?

We install wood privacy fences, cedar fences (vertical board-on-board and horizontal), ornamental iron fences, metal fences, chain link fences, ranch fences, split rail cedar fences, and agricultural fencing. We serve residential homeowners, commercial property owners, and agricultural landowners across all of Central Texas.

Do you install wood privacy fences and cedar fences?

Yes — cedar privacy fence installation is our most popular service. We use Western Red Cedar only (no pressure-treated pine) in both traditional vertical board-on-board and modern horizontal styles. We match your height requirements and HOA specs. Call (512) 710-7116 for a free estimate.

Do you install ornamental iron and metal fences?

Yes. We specialize in ornamental iron and metal fence installation for residential and commercial clients across Central Texas — from decorative front-yard iron fences to heavy-duty commercial perimeter fencing.

Do you install chain link fences?

Yes. We install chain link fences for residential, commercial, and industrial properties. Available in galvanized and vinyl-coated black, multiple heights and gauges. We also repair existing chain link.

Do you install horizontal cedar fences?

Yes. Horizontal board cedar is one of our most-requested styles and adds a modern, contemporary look to any property. Visit our gallery to see completed projects. Important: horizontal fences require proper steel post installation — wood posts will cause sagging over time.

Do you install gates?

Yes. We install custom gates — walk gates, driveway gates, and manual double-drive gates in wood, iron, metal, and chain link. We recommend welded metal frames for maximum durability. Call (512) 710-7116 or see our pricing guide for current gate pricing.

Do you handle fence repairs?

Yes. Fence repair is a core service. We repair storm-damaged fences, leaning posts, broken boards, sagging gates, and damaged chain link — with volume discounts for multiple post replacements. We'll always recommend the most cost-effective approach for your situation.

How much does fence installation cost in Austin, TX?

Fence costs vary based on material, linear footage, terrain, number of gates, and site conditions. Every property is different, which is why we offer free estimates. See our 2026 Austin fence pricing guide for detailed local numbers, or call (512) 710-7116 for a free on-site estimate.

Which areas do you serve?

We're based in Buda, TX and serve all communities within approximately 25 miles — including Austin, Kyle, Dripping Springs, San Marcos, Driftwood, Wimberley, Westlake Hills, Spicewood, Creedmoor, Del Valle, Sunset Valley, and Manchaca. Call to confirm your area.

Are you licensed and insured?

Texas does not require a state license for fence contractors. We carry $1,000,000 in liability insurance and back every fence installation with a 5-year workmanship warranty. You're protected — from the first post to the last board.

What does your 5-year workmanship warranty cover?

Our 5-year workmanship warranty covers installation-related issues under normal use. If something isn't right due to our workmanship, we'll come back and make it right at no charge.

How long does a typical fence install take?

Most standard residential fence projects are completed in one day. We set posts, build the fence, install gates, clean up, and do a final walkthrough with you before we leave. Large projects or unusual terrain may require two days — we'll tell you upfront.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. Free, no-obligation estimates on all projects. We'll walk your property, measure the line, and hand you a written quote — usually the same day you call. Visit budafenceco.com/contact or call (512) 710-7116.
